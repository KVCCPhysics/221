Practice Problem 1

A motorcyclist traveling along a straight, level road at a constant speed of \(10\text{m/s}\) tosses a stone straight upward relative to himself at \(3.0\text{ m/s}\). The stone is released from a height of \(1.3\text{ m}\) above the ground.

  1. Determine the initial launch angle (above the horizontal) of the stone as measured by a stationary observer on the sidewalk. [angle]
  2. Calculate the total horizontal distance the stone travels through the air before hitting the pavement as seen by the sidewalk observer. [m]
  3. Find the impact angle of the stone relative to the horizontal just before it strikes the ground. [angle]

Written Response: How far ahead of or behind the driver does the stone land when it strikes the ground? Assume the motorcycle continues at constant speed.

Practice Problem 2

During a tournament round, a golfer stands on the edge of a plateau overlooking a lowered green. The hole lies \(70\text{ m}\) horizontally downfield and \(20\text{ m}\) below the height of the ball at impact.If the shot is launched at an angle of \(40^\circ\) relative to the horizontal and lands directly in the cup without bouncing

  1. Find the required launch speed \(v_0\) of the ball [m/s].
  2. Find the impact speed at which the ball reaches the hole [m/s].

Written response: Would the golfer need to hit the ball with more or with less initial speed if the launch angle changed to \(45^\circ\)?

Practice Problem 3

  1. what is the total distance it has traveled after 1.1 seconds? [meters]
  2. What is the velocity of the rocket after 1.1 seconds? [m/s]
